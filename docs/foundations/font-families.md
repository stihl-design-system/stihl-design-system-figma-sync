# Font Families

## Font families

An optimized version of the STIHL Contraface has been developed for digital applications such as websites and apps. This is called **STIHL Contraface Digital** and comes in two separate families:

### STIHL Contraface Digital Display Title

This family is being used for big and short headlines that need to stand out.

**AA**

**ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ1234567890!”§$%&/()=?**

### STIHL Contraface Digital Text

This family is used for most elements - headlines, body texts and labels.

**Aa**

**AaBbCcDdEeFfGgHhIiJjKkLlMmNnOoPpQqRrSsTtUuVvWwXxYyZz1234567890!”§$%&/()=?**

### STIHL Contraface Digital Text Demi

This family is only used for flags.

**Aa**

**AaBbCcDdEeFfGgHhIiJjKkLlMmNnOoPpQqRrSsTtUuVvWwXxYyZz1234567890!”§$%&/()=?**
